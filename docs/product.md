# Product

> One paragraph: what this is, who it is for, and what they do today instead.

This document is the thing every PRD is measured against. It is not a roadmap
— what is in flight lives in the issue tracker — and not a pitch. One page. If
it reaches three, it has become a deck.

## Who this is for

The specific person. Their situation, not their demographics. "Everyone" here
has the same meaning it has in a PRD: the problem is not specific enough yet.

## The problem it exists for

The problem class, one level above any single feature. A PRD names one
instance of this; the product is the whole of it.

## What it is

What the thing is, in the user's words rather than the system's. Someone who
has not seen it should be able to repeat this back.
